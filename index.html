When Windshield Repair May Be Possible

A small, clean and stable chip may sometimes be repairable when it is away from the windshield edge and outside the driver's primary viewing area. A professional inspection is required before repair can be confirmed.

When Replacement May Be Necessary

Replacement may be recommended when the crack is spreading, reaches the windshield edge, affects visibility, damages multiple layers or interferes with a windshield-mounted camera.

ADAS Calibration

Many modern vehicles have cameras or sensors positioned near the windshield. Depending on the vehicle, calibration may be required after windshield replacement.

Mobile Auto-Glass Service in Kansas City

Before arranging mobile service, choose a legal and safe work location with enough space around the vehicle. Share the vehicle year, make, model, body style, ZIP code and windshield technology when requesting a quote.
